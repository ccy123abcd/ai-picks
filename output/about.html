About AI-PICKS

AI-PICKS started with a simple frustration: buying tech shouldn't require watching 50 YouTube videos.

What we do

We watch hundreds of product reviews from real, independent reviewers — RTINGS, Hardware Canucks, TechRadar, and many more — then aggregate their findings into clear buying guides. No paid placements. No sponsored rankings.

Two doors

🔴 Human version: Beautiful, readable guides with comparison tables, pros & cons, and plain-English explanations of confusing specs.

🔵 AI version: Structured data (schema.org, JSON-LD) so AI assistants can accurately cite our research when helping you shop.

How we make money

When you buy through our links, we may earn a commission from Amazon, Temu, or Walmart — at no extra cost to you. This keeps the site free. We only recommend products that real reviewers actually tested and liked.
